Short Layman Explanation (What is Intelligent RCA?)

Intelligent Root Cause Analysis (iRCA) means using AI to automatically find the real reason behind a problem, instead of engineers manually checking hundreds of alerts, logs, and dashboards.

In simple words:

iRCA connects the dots across network, cloud, and applications to explain what broke, why it broke, and where to fix it — in minutes, not hours.

1. Purpose & Vision

The purpose of iRCA is to automatically identify the most probable root cause of incidents across 5G RAN, 5G Core, Kubernetes CNFs, routers, and DevOps pipelines, and present it in a clear, explainable manner.

Vision:

Reduce MTTR dramatically by replacing manual war-room troubleshooting with AI-driven, evidence-based RCA.

2. Problem Statement

Current RCA processes:

  • Are manual and slow
  • Require multiple teams (NOC, SRE, DevOps, Network)
  • Suffer from alert storms and data silos
  • Do not scale with cloud-native 5G complexity

As a result:

  • Incidents take hours to resolve
  • Root causes are often guessed
  • Same issues repeat

3. Goals & Success Metrics

Goals

  • Automatically identify probable root cause
  • Correlate issues across network, cloud, and DevOps layers
  • Provide explainable and trusted RCA
  • Support faster and safer remediation

Success Metrics (KPIs)

  • ≥60% reduction in MTTR
  • ≥70% reduction in manual troubleshooting effort
  • ≥85% RCA accuracy
  • RCA generation time < 5 minutes

7. Functional Requirements

7.1 Correlation Engine

  • Correlate issues by time window
  • Map dependencies across layers
  • Identify blast radius

7.2 RCA Intelligence

  • Build causal relationship graphs
  • Rank probable root causes
  • Assign confidence scores

7.3 Explainability

  • Explain why a root cause is selected
  • Show supporting evidence (metrics/logs)
  • Maintain RCA traceability

7.4 Human-in-the-Loop

  • Allow engineers to validate or override RCA
  • Capture feedback for learning

8. LLM-Based RCA Explanation (Guarded)

  • Convert RCA output into human-readable explanation
  • Use evidence-only context
  • Enforce confidence thresholds
  • Prevent hallucination
