2. Purpose of the Document

This PRD defines the business, functional, technical, safety, and compliance requirements for deploying a DGMS‑compliant Private 5G Network in underground and opencast coal mines. The objective is to enhance worker safety, operational efficiency, automation readiness, and regulatory compliance through real‑time connectivity and intelligent monitoring.

 

3. Business Problem Statement

Coal mine operations currently suffer from:

�        Limited and unreliable underground connectivity

�        Delayed detection of gas, fire, and roof‑fall hazards

�        High human exposure in hazardous zones

�        Manual equipment monitoring and reactive maintenance

�        Fragmented systems and limited real‑time visibility

�        Manual, audit‑heavy DGMS compliance processes

These challenges lead to safety risks, production loss, higher operating costs, and regulatory exposure.
