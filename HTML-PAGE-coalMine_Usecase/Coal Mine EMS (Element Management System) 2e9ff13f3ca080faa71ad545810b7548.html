Coal Mine EMS (Element Management System)

Solution Design Document – DGMS & 5G-Ready

1. Introduction

The Coal Mine EMS is the central system responsible for managing, monitoring, controlling, and automating all mine elements such as sensors, machines, cameras, wearables, drones, and safety systems over a 5G Private Network.

The EMS acts as the single source of truth for mine operations and safety, while integrating with NMS (network health) and AI-RCA (intelligent decision-making).

2. EMS Design Philosophy (Mining-First)

  1. Safety-first, not IT-first
  1. Works in harsh underground conditions
  1. Simple for operators, powerful underneath
  1. DGMS-compliant by default
  1. Fail-safe & redundant

3. EMS Scope & Responsibilities

EMS Owns:

  • Devices, machines, sensors
  • Safety logic & automation
  • Alerts & alarms
  • Data collection & logs
  • DGMS reports

EMS Does NOT Own:

  • 5G radio & core health (NMS)
  • Deep root cause reasoning (AI-RCA)

Layers

5. Managed Elements (What EMS Controls)

5.1 Safety & Environmental Devices

  • Multi-gas sensors (CH₄, CO, O₂)
  • Fire & smoke detectors
  • Dust, temperature, humidity sensors
  • Sirens & evacuation alarms
  • Roof movement & vibration sensors

5.2 Miner Wearables

  • Smart helmets (camera, SOS)
  • Location tags
  • Health sensors
  • Smart cap lamps

5.3 Cameras & Vision Systems

  • AI CCTV cameras
  • Thermal cameras
  • PTZ cameras
  • 360° cameras

5.4 Machinery & OT Equipment

  • Conveyor belts
  • Drillers & cutters
  • Loaders & dumpers
  • Cranes
  • Hydraulic hammers
  • Ventilation fans

5.5 Drones & Robotics

  • Inspection drones
  • Thermal drones
  • Emergency reconnaissance robots

6. EMS Core Functional Modules

6.1 Device Lifecycle Management

  • Auto discovery & onboarding
  • Provisioning & configuration
  • Firmware & software updates
  • Health & heartbeat monitoring

6.2 Telemetry & Data Ingestion

  • High-frequency sensor data
  • Video streams (real-time & stored)
  • Event & alarm data
  • Control feedback

Protocols supported:

  • MQTT
  • REST / HTTPS
  • OPC-UA
  • RTSP (video)

6.3 Real-Time Monitoring

  • Live gas & environment dashboards
  • Miner location heatmaps
  • Machine status views
  • Zone-wise safety indicators

6.4 Rules Engine (Safety Logic)

Examples:

  • IF methane > threshold → siren ON
  • IF temperature rising + vibration → conveyor shutdown
  • IF miner enters restricted zone → alert
  • IF no movement + high temp → fatigue alert

6.5 Control & Automation

  • Remote start/stop of machines
  • Ventilation fan speed control
  • Conveyor auto-shutdown
  • Emergency broadcast alerts
EMS automation always follows DGMS-approved logic

7. EMS Interfaces – How Everything Connects

7.1 Device → EMS

Device TypeInterface
SensorsMQTT / 5G CPE
Wearables5G / BLE → Gateway
CamerasRTSP / ONVIF
MachinesPLC → OPC-UA
Drones5G IP Stream

7.2 EMS → Machines (Control)

  • Secure command APIs
  • Role-based authorization
  • Manual override support

7.3 EMS ↔ NMS Integration

  • EMS queries network health
  • EMS adapts QoS needs (emergency priority)

7.4 EMS ↔ AI-RCA Integration

  • EMS sends alarms & telemetry
  • AI-RCA sends root cause & recommendations
  • EMS executes approved actions

8. Alarm & Incident Management

Alarm Lifecycle

  1. Detection
  1. Correlation
  1. Severity classification
  1. Notification (siren, app, SMS)
  1. Acknowledgement
  1. Closure

9. DGMS Compliance & Reporting

Mandatory Reports

  • Gas concentration trends
  • Fire & incident logs
  • Worker attendance & movement
  • Emergency response timeline

Formats:

  • PDF
  • CSV
  • Digital signatures

10. Security & Access Control

  • Role-based access (RBAC)
  • Device authentication
  • Encrypted communication
  • Audit trails

11. High Availability & Fail-Safe

  • Edge-first operation
  • Local decision even if WAN fails
  • Battery-backed alarms
  • Manual override for machines

12. EMS KPIs

  • Mean time to detect (MTTD)
  • Mean time to respond (MTTR)
  • Safety incident reduction
  • Device uptime
  • False alarm rate

13. Deployment Model

Phase 1 – Safety Core

  • Gas, fire, wearables

Phase 2 – Operations

  • Machines, conveyors, cameras

Phase 3 – Intelligence

  • AI-RCA, predictive analytics

14. Summary

The Coal Mine EMS is the operational brain of a 5G-enabled mine, ensuring:

  • Miner safety
  • Operational efficiency
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Automation with accountability
If NMS is the nervous system and AI-RCA is the brain, EMS is the heart of the digital mine.
