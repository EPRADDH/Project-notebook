Responsible to Build a 5G-Enabled Coal Mine

Key Points  Will Consider Before Using 5G

1. Mine Reality (Not Lab Conditions)

  • Dust, humidity, vibration
  • Low light, heat
  • Narrow tunnels & metal interference
  • Frequent power cuts

2. DGMS & Indian Mine Rules

  • Gas detection & logging is mandatory
  • Emergency communication must work always
  • Manual inspection records are audited
  • Worker tracking during emergencies

3. Miner Life & Daily Pain Points

What miners struggle with today:

  • No early gas warning
  • No idea who is inside during emergency
  • Manual inspection in dangerous areas
  • Slow rescue coordination
  • Fatigue, heat, poor visibility

4. What 5G Actually Solves in Coal Mines

ProblemHow 5G Helps
