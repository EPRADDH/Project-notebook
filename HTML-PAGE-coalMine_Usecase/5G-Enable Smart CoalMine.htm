Executive Summary

Project: 5G-Enabled Smart Coal Mine (Next-Gen Digital Mine)

Project Overview

The 5G-Enabled Smart Coal Mine Project is a strategic digital transformation initiative aimed at modernizing coal mining operations by leveraging private 5G connectivity, AI-driven analytics, industrial IoT, and automation.

The project establishes a secure, high-reliability digital backbone that enables real-time monitoring, intelligent decision-making, and remote or autonomous operations across underground and opencast mining environments.

By integrating safety systems, equipment, workforce tracking, and operational data into a unified platform, the project seeks to enhance worker safety, improve productivity, reduce operational risk, and ensure regulatory compliance, while preparing the mine for future automation and sustainability requirements.

Coal mining faces persistent challenges:

  • High safety risks (gas, roof fall, equipment accidents)
  • Low operational visibility in underground environments
  • Equipment downtime and manual dependency
  • Compliance pressure from DGMS, MoC, and environmental bodies

Coal mines need:

  • Ultra-reliable connectivity (no public network dependency)
  • Low latency (real-time control & safety)
  • High bandwidth (video, VR, AI)
  • Secure & isolated network

 

Scope of the Project

The project covers:

  • Deployment of a private 5G network across mine premises (underground and surface)
  • Sensorization of critical safety, environmental, and operational parameters
  • Real-time tracking of workforce and assets
  • Integration of connected mining equipment and telematics
  • AI-driven safety monitoring, predictive maintenance, and operational analytics
  • Establishment of a Centralized Mine Command & Control Center
  • Automated compliance, reporting, and ESG monitoring

 

Key Outcomes

  • Reduced human exposure in hazardous zones
  • Real-time visibility into safety, production, and equipment health
  • Improved asset utilization and reduced unplanned downtime
  • Faster, data-driven operational decisions
  • Digitally enabled regulatory compliance
  • Scalable foundation for future automation and autonomous mining

Business Problem / Opportunity

Business Problem

Coal mining operations today face significant safety, efficiency, and compliance challenges due to limited real-time visibility, fragmented operational systems, and heavy dependence on manual processes�especially in underground and high-risk environments.

Key issues include:

  • High safety risk and human exposure to hazards such as gas leakage, roof falls, equipment collisions, and ventilation failures, leading to accidents, production stoppages, and regulatory scrutiny.
  • Lack of real-time operational intelligence, with delayed or siloed data across safety systems, equipment, workforce tracking, and production monitoring.
  • Unplanned equipment downtime and low asset utilization, driven by reactive maintenance practices and limited condition monitoring.
  • Inefficient coordination of people, machines, and processes, resulting in suboptimal production output and higher operating costs.
  • Manual, time-consuming regulatory compliance and reporting, increasing the risk of errors, non-compliance, and audit findings.
  • Limited scalability of legacy communication systems (VHF, Wi-Fi, wired networks) that cannot support advanced automation, video analytics, or AI-driven use cases.

 

Business Opportunity

Advances in private 5G, edge computing, AI, and industrial IoT present a strategic opportunity to redefine coal mining operations by enabling a connected, intelligent, and safer mining ecosystem.

This opportunity enables the organization to:

  • Significantly improve worker safety by enabling real-time hazard detection, predictive alerts, and remote operations that reduce human presence in dangerous zones.
  • Increase productivity and operational efficiency through real-time monitoring, AI-driven optimization, and autonomous or semi-autonomous mining operations.
  • Reduce operational costs and downtime via predictive maintenance, better asset utilization, and optimized energy consumption.
  • Ensure proactive regulatory compliance through automated data capture, traceability, and audit-ready reporting.
  • Create a future-ready digital mine platform that supports automation, digital twins, and ESG-driven sustainability goals.
  • Establish a competitive and technological leadership position by adopting 5G-enabled smart mining ahead of industry peers.

 

�Vision and Strategic Objective

�       The primary goal of 5G integration is to achieve "safety, fewer people, and unmanned mining". This transformation addresses the industry's historical struggles with worker safety, environmental impact, and operational inefficiency by moving personnel away from dangerous working surfaces to secure Remote Operating Centers (ROCs)

 

�Core Technology Infrastructure

�       Independent Private Network: Large-scale mines utilize an independent core network physically isolated from public networks to ensure maximum security and data confidentiality,

�       Tailored Connectivity: To overcome the challenges of rugged terrain and deep tunnels, mines deploy a combination of directional radios for range and radiating cables (leaky feeders) to maintain a constant "Line of Sight" (LOS) in underground drifts

�       Movable Infrastructure: In open-pit scenarios where the working surface advances, movable trailer-type base stations ensure continuous coverage.

�       Edge Computing (MEC): Data is processed locally at the mine site, reducing latency from 300 ms to less than 80 ms, which is critical for the real-time control of heavy machinery

Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is to implement a 5G-enabled Smart Mining Platform that integrates private 5G connectivity, industrial IoT, AI/ML analytics, and centralized operational management to transform coal mine operations into a safe, intelligent, and highly efficient digital ecosystem.

At the core of the solution is a private 5G network that serves as the unified digital backbone, enabling reliable, low-latency, and secure communication between workers, machines, sensors, and enterprise systems�both underground and on the surface.

1. Private 5G Digital Backbone

  • Deploy a dedicated private 5G network covering underground workings, surface facilities, and control rooms
  • Support ultra-low latency, high-bandwidth, and high-availability communications
  • Enable network slicing for:

�       Safety-critical traffic

�       Video and surveillance

�       Equipment control and automation

2. Smart Safety & Environment Monitoring

  • Deploy IoT sensors for gas (CH₄, CO, O₂), temperature, ventilation, strata movement, and water levels
  • 5G-enabled wearables for worker location tracking, fall detection, and emergency alerts
  • AI-based anomaly detection and predictive hazard alerts
  • Automated evacuation and response workflows

3. Connected Equipment & Asset Intelligence

  • Integrate mining equipment (continuous miners, excavators, conveyors, haulage vehicles) via telematics
  • Real-time monitoring of equipment health, utilization, and energy consumption
  • Remote diagnostics and condition-based maintenance

4. AI-Driven Operations & Predictive Maintenance

  • Predictive maintenance models using vibration, thermal, and operational data
  • AI-assisted production planning and dispatch optimization
  • Early fault detection and automated maintenance work orders

5. Remote & Semi-Autonomous Operations

  • Enable remote operation of critical equipment from a safe control room
  • Pilot autonomous or semi-autonomous haulage and drilling in defined zones
  • Reduce manpower exposure in high-risk areas

6. Centralized Mine Command & Control Center

  • Establish an Integrated Mine Command & Control Center (IMCCC)
  • Single dashboard for:

�       Safety status

�       Production KPIs

�       Equipment health

�       Workforce movement

�       Energy and environmental metrics

  • Digital Twin for scenario simulation and emergency preparedness
