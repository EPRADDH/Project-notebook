Miner Day-in-Life : Before 5G vs After 5G-Enabled Coal Mine

1. Start of Shift (Reporting & Entry)

BEFORE 5G

  • Miner reports at shift office
  • Manual attendance register
  • Safety briefing is verbal
  • Gas status reported from previous shift
  • No real-time visibility of underground conditions

AFTER 5G

  • Miner issued smart helmet + wearable
  • Automatic attendance when entering mine
  • Live gas, fire & ventilation status on control room
  • Helmet vibrates if area is unsafe
  • Supervisor sees who is entering which zone

2. Travel to Work Face (Underground Movement)

BEFORE 5G

  • Dark tunnels, limited visibility
  • No tracking of miner movement
  • If miner collapses, no one knows exact location
  • Communication via wired phones (if available)

AFTER 5G

  • Miner location tracked in real-time
  • Smart cap lamp auto-adjusts light
  • Control room sees live movement map
  • SOS button on helmet works anywhere

3. Active Working Hours (Drilling, Cutting, Transport)

BEFORE 5G

  • Manual drilling with no live monitoring
  • Conveyor belt inspected physically
  • Dust and heat levels guessed
  • Unsafe behavior noticed only after incident

AFTER 5G

  • Drillers monitored remotely
  • Conveyor belts auto-alert before fire or jam
  • Dust, heat, gas tracked continuously
  • AI camera detects unsafe PPE usage

4. Emergency Scenario (Gas Leak / Fire)

BEFORE 5G

  • Gas detected late
  • Manual siren activation
  • No idea how many miners are inside
  • Rescue team searches blindly

AFTER 5G

  • Gas threshold crossed → instant alert (<10 ms)
  • Sirens auto-activate
  • Ventilation increases automatically
  • Control room sees exact miner locations
  • Rescue guided by live data

5. Inspection & Risk Zones

BEFORE 5G

  • Supervisors enter unstable areas
  • Post-blast inspection done manually
  • DGMS inspection requires physical visit

AFTER 5G

  • Drones inspect tunnels & post-blast zones
  • Thermal cameras detect hotspots
  • DGMS can do virtual inspection (VR mine)

6. End of Shift (Exit & Reporting)

BEFORE 5G

  • Manual exit register
  • Verbal incident reporting
  • Data scattered across logbooks
  • Delayed analysis

AFTER 5G

  • Automatic exit detection
  • Shift safety & production report auto-generated
  • DGMS-ready digital logs
  • AI highlights risks for next shift
